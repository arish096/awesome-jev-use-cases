# Privacy policy: Slop Blocker for LinkedIn

Last updated 2026-09-20.

- The extension reads the text and author name of posts on linkedin.com/feed while you browse.
- The author name (a name that can identify a person) is sent together with the post text when the Jev layer is on.
- If you add a TypeSafe API key and enable the Jev layer or the tagger, that post text is sent from your browser directly to api.typesafe.ai using your key. TypeSafe's terms and privacy policy apply to that data.
- Without a key, nothing leaves your device.
- Your TypeSafe API key is a credential. It is stored in your browser's local extension storage, is not synced, and is sent only to api.typesafe.ai as your authorization header.
- Settings, categories and learned corrections are stored in Chrome sync storage so they follow your Chrome profile.
- The extension has no analytics, no ads and no server of its own. The developer does not receive your data.
- To delete everything, remove the extension.
